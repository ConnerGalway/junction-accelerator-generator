About

A junction is where routes meet. So are we.

Junction sits where organizations and technology meet, and where consulting meets training. Fourteen years in the visitor economy, helping destinations and the businesses inside them get measurably better at marketing. In 2026 our training platform, eLearningU, became JunctionU and moved in under one roof.

What we believe

01

The best engagement is one you never have to repeat.

Every plan is built for handover. Every program is built to be owned by the people who run it. Your team should need us less every month.

02

Independence keeps advice honest.

We only do strategy and training. Since nothing we recommend earns us a commission, everything we recommend is there on merit.

03

Plain language, always.

If an idea cannot be explained to a busy operator between two guest check-ins, it needs more work. Jargon usually means someone is hiding.
