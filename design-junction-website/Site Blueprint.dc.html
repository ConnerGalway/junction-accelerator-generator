Pages, one by one

HomeJob: recognition + routing in 10 seconds

Massive-type hero with the converging-routes motion graphic. Two doors (the plan / the people). Proof band: named clients + three numbers. One tool teaser each for operators and DMOs. Latest thinking + Brief capture. Footer CTA to /start.

Needs from you: final headline sign-off, 6–8 client names cleared for use, hero stat choices.
Services hub + 6 service pagesJob: qualify and route

Hub sells the two doors, then routes. Each service page follows one template: who it's for, what you get, how it runs, what it costs (Accelerator $2,500 and course prices public; consulting is enquiry-only), one relevant case study, one relevant tool, enquiry CTA pre-routed. Speaking page adds signature talks, past stages, and a short event-details form.

Needs from you: engagement models per service (typical length, deliverables), speaking headshots/footage.
AI Training — the 30-day program /services/aiJob: 1 non-tourism enquiry/week

A productized page, sold like the Accelerator. What you get in 30 days: 4 training sessions, a custom training dashboard, a prompt library your team keeps, a 90-day implementation plan, and at least one business bottleneck automated before the program ends. That last deliverable is the headline — it's a guarantee, and nobody else offers it. Day-by-day timeline, who it's for (marketing teams in and beyond tourism), and its own CTA on the homepage. Enquiry route pre-set to AI training.

Needs from you: program price or "from" range, a real example of an automated bottleneck, dashboard screenshots when ready.
Work + interactive case studiesJob: proof for validators

Ontario's Southwest, Travel Yukon Go Digital, Indigenous Tourism BC, Kootenay Rockies. Each: the situation, what we did, numbers that moved, a quote, and one explorable artifact (a flip-through plan, a scrubbable program timeline, a before/after score). Case studies cross-link to the service that produced them.

Status: all four cleared with names and numbers. You have more case studies to add — send the list and each gets the same template. Still needed: one real (anonymizable) deliverable per case.
JunctionU — catalogue, course pages, dashboardJob: daily free signups

Catalogue with filtering (certificates / workshops / free). Course pages with module lists, instructor, price, free preview module. Checkout happens on-site, card to certificate without leaving the page. Free tier (locked): Tourism Talks + one starter mini-course, account required — the product-led front door. Learner dashboard: progress, weekly action, certificates. DMO group view (seats, member progress) in phase 2.

Needs from you: full course list with prices; payment account (Stripe or similar) for on-site checkout.
Ideas — articles + The BriefJob: the lead magnet at scale

Your content volume, organized: filterable article library (AI / marketing / tourism / trends), each article ending in one relevant next step (a tool, a course, or /start — matched to topic, never generic). Brief signup on every article, plus a Brief archive page that shows what subscribers actually get. With 100+ articles migrating, this is the SEO engine: every article gets a keyword-mapped title, meta description, clean slug, structured data and internal links, and every one ends in a Brief signup. Target queries from "tourism marketing consultant" down to the long tail each article already answers.

Needs from you: the 100+ article export, category call, 3 sample Brief issues, target keyword list if one exists.
AboutJob: make the intro email answerable

The Junction story (including eLearningU becoming JunctionU), what we believe, Conner's bio with real photography, the no-conflicts position stated plainly.

Needs from you: photography, the founding story in your words, team roster if more than Conner.
/start — Start a conversationJob: the primary conversion

One routed form: who you are (DMO / operator / other org / event organizer) → what you're after → goal in a sentence → timeline and range. Each route lands in your inbox tagged and pre-qualified. Qualified routes land on an embedded calendar so the call books itself on the spot; the confirmation offers The Brief while they wait. Every CTA on the site ends here, pre-filled with its route.

The two tools, specced

Digital Snapshot /tools/snapshot

Operator answers ~12 yes/no questions from the Accelerator's audit checklist (Google profile complete? Replied to your last 5 reviews? Site loads under 3s on mobile?). Instant score out of 100 by category, one free fix shown in full. Full report + fix list arrives by email. Score bands route the CTA: low → Accelerator, mid → a course, high → The Brief. Weekly digest of completions to you.

Program Builder /tools/program-builder

DMO enters operator count, subsidy level (25–100%) and program goals. Output: modelled program with seat costs, projected completions (based on your real completion rates), and economic framing grounded in the Okotoks $900K and Yukon 300-business numbers. Exports a board-ready one-pager PDF with their region's name on it. Ends at /start, route pre-set to DMO, inputs attached.

Cross-site systems

Brief captureOne component, everywhere: end of articles, tool results, footers. Shows subscriber count and a real sample issue.
Next-step bridgesEvery article and case study ends with one matched next step. Mapping lives in one config file so you can retune it without redesign.
Proof registryClient names, numbers and quotes kept in one data file, drawn on by every page — update once, correct everywhere.
MeasurementEach 12-month target gets an event: enquiry by route, JunctionU signup, Brief signup, tool completion, speaking enquiry. A simple private /metrics page shows weekly counts against your targets.
