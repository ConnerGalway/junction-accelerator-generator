About

A junction is where routes meet. So are we.

Junction sits where organizations and technology meet — and where consulting meets training. For fourteen years we've worked in the visitor economy, helping destinations and the businesses inside them get measurably better at marketing. In 2026, we brought our training platform, eLearningU, fully into the fold as JunctionU.

What we believe

Capability over dependency.

The best engagement is one you don't need to repeat. Every plan is built to be handed over; every program is built to be owned by the people who run it.

No conflicts of interest.

We don't build websites, manage social media, or buy ads. When we recommend something, it's because it works — not because we sell it.

Plain language, always.

A no-jargon zone. If an idea can't be explained to a busy operator between two guest check-ins, it isn't ready.
