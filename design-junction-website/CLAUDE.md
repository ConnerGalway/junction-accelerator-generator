# Junction website project — persistent instructions

## Voice (applies to ALL site copy)
Casual, direct, confident, friendly. Plain-spoken, specific, numbers over adjectives.

## Hard rules — avoid AI-writing tells (per Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing")
- NO negative parallelisms / contrast-reframes: "It's not X, it's Y", "X, not Y", "We don't X. We Y." Say the thing directly instead.
- No rule-of-three adjective triplets ("innovative, practical, and proven").
- No puffery or significance-editorializing ("it's important to note", "truly", "seamless", "transformative").
- No AI vocabulary: delve, intricate, tapestry, pivotal, underscore, landscape, foster, testament, enhance, crucial, elevate, unlock, empower, journey (except literal travel), robust.
- No em-dash overuse for punchy emphasis; prefer commas or periods.
- No excessive bolding of key terms.
- No vague attributions ("experts agree", "studies show") — name the client, the number, the event.
- Vary sentence rhythm; concrete details over generalities; it's fine to admit something is hard or boring.

## Brand decisions (locked Aug 2026)
- Deep green #114B3C is the Junction colour; warm paper #F5F1E8 surface; near-black ink #171512; gold #f2a900 strictly for CTAs/action. Orange retired.
- Type: Overpass — heavy (800/900) for big declarative moments, light (300) for advisory copy, Overpass Mono for small wayfinding labels only.
- Wayfinding concept survives as structure and language (routes, numbered services, converging-routes motion), never as sign-panel costume. Max one signature motif per page.
- Primary conversion: qualified "Start a conversation" enquiry form, routed by audience (DMO / operator / AI training / speaking).
- Lead engines: Digital Snapshot (operator assessment), Program Builder (DMO ROI calculator), interactive case studies.
