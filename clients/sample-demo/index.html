Social Media Activation

Build a consistent, engaging presence that drives discovery and converts followers into guests.

What This Is

A four-week sprint to transform your social media from dormant to active. You will establish a posting rhythm, repurpose your existing photography into engaging content, launch short-form video, and build the engagement habits that grow an audience organically.

What This Breaks Into
Content Calendar
Cabin features Monday, local activities Wednesday, guest experiences Friday
Batch Creation
4 weeks of posts in one sitting using your 37 existing photos
Instagram Reels
2-3 short videos per week showcasing cabin views and cozy moments
Engagement Habits
Respond within 2 hours, follow local accounts, use location hashtags
What We Heard From You

"You have beautiful cabin imagery and an excellent guest experience, but your social media presence isn't capturing this value. With only 522 followers and posting just once every 10 weeks, you're missing opportunities to reach new guests."

Why It Matters

Leisure travellers discover accommodations through Instagram and Facebook before they ever search Google. A dormant social presence signals a dormant business. Three posts a week for twelve straight weeks beats a burst of ten posts followed by a month of silence.

Time Commitment
2 hrs per week for content + engagement
  1. 1
    Set Up Content Calendar
    • Create a repeatable posting schedule: cabin features on Monday, local activities on Wednesday, guest experiences on Friday.
    • Use a free tool like Later or the Meta Business Suite to schedule posts in advance.
    • Block 30 minutes each Sunday to plan the week ahead.
  2. 2
    Batch Create Content
    • Use your existing 37 website photos to create 4 weeks of posts in one sitting.
    • Write captions that tell stories, not just describe the image.
    • Prepare 12-16 posts at once so you are never scrambling for content.
  1. 3
    Launch Instagram Reels
    • Create 2-3 short video Reels per week showcasing cabin views, nature sounds, and cozy moments.
    • Reels get 3-5x more reach than static posts, so prioritize short video content.
    • Keep videos under 30 seconds. Show, don't tell.
  2. 4
    Engage Consistently
    • Respond to comments within 2 hours during business hours.
    • Follow local tourism accounts, restaurants, and activity providers.
    • Use 5-10 relevant hashtags: #lakesidecabin #cabinlife #naturegetaway #weekendretreat

What to Measure vs. What to Ignore

Follower count is a vanity metric. Focus on engagement rate (likes + comments / followers) and website clicks. A smaller, engaged audience converts better than a large, passive one.

Quick Tips
  • Post when your audience is active: 7-9am and 7-9pm for leisure travellers.
  • Use 5-10 relevant hashtags: #lakesidecabin #cabinlife #naturegetaway #weekendretreat
  • Reels get 3-5x more reach than static posts. Prioritize short video content.
  • Respond to every comment. Engagement begets engagement.

Review Generation

Build social proof that converts browsers into bookers with a systematic approach to reviews.

What This Is

A systematic approach to turning satisfied guests into vocal advocates. You will set up automated review requests, deploy physical reminders in your cabins, establish a response protocol, and expand your presence across multiple review platforms.

What This Breaks Into
Email System
Automated request 3 days post-checkout with direct Google link
QR Cards
Physical cards in cabins linking directly to review page
Response Protocol
Reply to every review within 24 hours with personalized thanks
Platform Expansion
Claim TripAdvisor and encourage reviews for broader visibility
What We Heard From You

"You have a perfect 5.0 Google rating, but only 20 reviews. This limits your visibility in local search results and doesn't reflect the volume of happy guests you've hosted."

Why It Matters

Review count is a ranking factor. A property with 70 reviews at 4.8 stars outranks a property with 20 reviews at 5.0 stars. Volume matters as much as quality, and recency matters most of all.

Time Commitment
30 min per week for responses + monitoring
  1. 1
    Create Review Request System
    • Set up automated email 3 days post-checkout with direct link to Google review page.
    • Use your booking system or a tool like Mailchimp to automate the sequence.
    • Keep the email short: thank them, ask for feedback, provide the link.
  2. 2
    Deploy QR Code Cards
    • Place review request cards in each cabin with QR code linking directly to Google reviews.
    • Position near checkout instructions or on the welcome packet.
    • Keep the design simple: "Loved your stay? Leave us a review!"
  1. 3
    Respond to All Reviews
    • Reply to every review within 24 hours with personalized thanks.
    • Mention specific details from their stay to show you remember them.
    • Include an invitation to return: "We hope to see you again soon!"
  2. 4
    Expand to Other Platforms
    • Claim and optimize your TripAdvisor listing.
    • Add TripAdvisor as a second option in your review request email.
    • Consider Booking.com and Airbnb if you list on those platforms.

What to Measure vs. What to Ignore

Focus on review velocity (new reviews per month) rather than just total count. A steady stream of recent reviews signals an active business. Ignore negative reviews that are clearly fake or off-topic.

Quick Tips
  • The best time to ask for a review is 3-5 days after checkout when memories are fresh.
  • Make it easy: Include a direct link that takes guests straight to the review form.
  • Personalize responses by mentioning specific details from their stay.
  • Never offer incentives for reviews. It violates platform policies and erodes trust.

Local SEO Optimization

Capture high-intent searchers ready to book

Timeline: Weeks 9-12 Phase: Scale

What We Heard From You

Your Google Business Profile is verified but underutilized. You're missing Local Business Schema on your website, and your backlink profile is limited. These gaps are costing you visibility when potential guests search for "cabin rentals near me" or "lakeside getaway."

What This Tactic Involves

  • Optimize your Google Business Profile with photos, posts, and Q&A
  • Implement LocalBusiness and LodgingBusiness schema markup
  • Build local citations on 20+ directories with consistent NAP
  • Create local content about attractions and area guides

  1. Upload new photos weekly

    Add high-quality photos of cabins, amenities, and scenic views to keep your profile fresh

  2. Create Google Posts

    Post weekly updates about seasonal offerings, special packages, or local events

  3. Monitor and respond to Q&A

    Answer questions within 24 hours to show active engagement

  4. Keep business info current

    Update hours, services, and amenities whenever they change

  1. Add LocalBusiness schema

    Implement structured data with your business name, address, phone, and hours

  2. Add LodgingBusiness schema

    Include accommodation-specific details like amenities and star rating

  3. Validate with Google Rich Results Test

    Test your schema implementation to ensure it's properly detected

  1. Audit existing citations

    Find and fix any inconsistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across existing listings

  2. Submit to major directories

    List on Yelp, TripAdvisor, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and industry-specific sites

  3. Build local directory listings

    Submit to regional tourism boards, chambers of commerce, and local business associations

  1. Write local attraction guides

    Create blog posts about nearby hiking, fishing, restaurants, and activities

  2. Publish seasonal content

    Create guides for each season highlighting relevant activities and events

  3. Optimize for local keywords

    Target phrases like "cabin rentals in [area]" and "[area] vacation rentals"
