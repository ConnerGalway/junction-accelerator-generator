# Lifty Life — Accelerator Implementation Plan

## Meta
Client Slug: lifty-life
Has GBP Quick Wins: yes

## Goal

Lifty Life manages 22 homes in Harrison Hot Springs and 25 more in the surrounding region, and almost all of them are sold by someone else. In 2026, Airbnb produced 50 percent of Harrison revenue, VRBO 29 percent and Booking.com 9 percent. Direct bookings, through your website and Google Vacation Rentals, made up about 11 percent. At an average commission of around 15 percent, that mix sends roughly $100,000 a year to the platforms. You put the trade-off plainly: on a $1,000 booking, $150 goes to Airbnb, and a $50 cost to win that booking yourself would be a far better deal.

The goal you set for the Accelerator is to move Harrison direct bookings from about 11 percent toward 30 percent. That is the right destination, and on the coaching call you and Conner agreed it is a 2027 number rather than a 90-day one. Most of the direct bookings you get today are repeat guests who came back to save the service fees. Nothing currently gives a first-time guest a reason to choose Lifty Life over the same home on Airbnb, and most guests check out without knowing who Lifty Life is. The job of the next ninety days is to fix that: give booking direct a real set of benefits, put those benefits in front of every guest you are allowed to reach, and start buying the Harrison searches the big platforms ignore.

The plan does not ask you to build a members' portal. A "Lifty Life Member" for the next ninety days is simply someone on your email list who gets better terms when they book direct. A tag in your email platform and a custom field in Hostaway do the work a login system would do later. If the idea earns its keep, the portal becomes a sensible 2027 project with real member numbers behind it.

The order matters. Weeks 1 to 5 define the benefits and publish them on the website with a sign-up form, because every email and ad later in the plan points at that page. Weeks 3 to 11 build the follow-up that brings direct guests and members back for their next stay. Weeks 3 to 10 build an extended stays page and a small outbound list for the off-season, while the Agassiz pipeline work and insurance relocations are live demand. Google Ads tracking goes in during Week 2, and the campaigns switch on in Weeks 8 and 9, once there is a page worth sending people to and a way to count the bookings.

Stated honestly, the direct share will move slowly inside these ninety days. October to January is your quiet season in Harrison, and much of next summer gets booked in the spring. What the ninety days should produce is a member list that grows every week, the first next-year rebookings from guests who checked out this autumn, the first extended-stay bookings from outbound work, and a cost-per-booking figure from Google Ads you can compare against the $150 commission. Those are also the numbers that matter most for the part of the business you care about most. A property owner deciding between Lifty Life and another manager will be far more persuaded by "a growing share of our bookings come direct, and we fill your calendar in October" than by a lower fee.

## Strategic Positioning

On every booking platform, a Lifty Life home sits beside thousands of others, and the guest's choice is about the property and the price. Your own site currently makes the same offer: here is the home, pick where to book it. That puts you in a direct fight with Airbnb and VRBO for the same guest, and as you said on the call, taking that fight to them is expensive and highly competitive.

The thing they cannot match is that you are local and you are present. Guests reach you or your guest services manager, people who have stood in the property, rather than a support centre overseas. You have bent cancellation terms for direct guests when it made sense. You can offer early check-in or late checkout when the calendar allows, monthly payments for a contractor staying two months, and first choice of a lake-view home for next August. Reviewers already notice this. The recurring themes in your 78 Google reviews are location, value, the full kitchen and attentive communication. None of that is currently said anywhere a guest would read it before booking.

The Harrison opportunity is also different from your ski markets. There are many hotels in town and few property managers, and you believe your homes are better than most of the hotel stock. Someone searching for somewhere to stay in Harrison Hot Springs, who has not already chosen Airbnb, is comparing you with tired hotel rooms, and that is a comparison you win. The name "Lifty Life" does not need to explain Harrison by itself. A plain descriptor beside it, such as "locally managed homes in Harrison Hot Springs," does that job.

> **Your Strategic Positioning**
>
> Lifty Life is the local team behind some of the best homes in Harrison Hot Springs and across BC. Book with us and you get the same home with better terms: real people who know the property, more flexible policies, and first access to the homes and dates our members want most.

## Tactic 1: Define and Publish the Lifty Life Guest Benefits
Phase: Foundation
Short Label: Guest Benefits
Subtitle: Turn the perks you already give direct guests case by case into a published set of benefits, with a page and sign-up form that make anyone a Lifty Life Member.

### What We Heard From You

You told us that, today, the reason to book direct is purely about price: guests skip the platform service fees, which can be as much as 15 percent. You also described perks you already give, but only occasionally: free cancellation where an OTA booking would not have qualified, an early check-in or late checkout, and sometimes a bottle of wine. You were cautious about advertising those terms because they have always been handled case by case. And you pointed out that, across this many markets, anything needing a site visit, like organising a cleaner to drop off wine, quickly becomes a logistics problem. You also said the site was built for homeowners rather than guests, and that visitors often assume Lifty Life is just another OTA.

### What This Is

Three pieces of work. First, choosing four or five benefits your team can deliver from a desk in Harrison with no extra trips to properties, and writing them down so they stop being case by case. Second, building a single Benefits page on liftylife.ca that explains who Lifty Life is, what members get and how to join, with an email sign-up form. Third, putting a short version of that message on the homepage and through the booking path, so a visitor understands you are the local manager, not a booking platform.

### Why It Matters

Everything else in this plan points at this page. The welcome emails in Tactic 2 explain it, the post-stay emails invite guests to use it, and the Google Ads in Tactic 4 send traffic to it. If the benefits stay informal, none of those messages has anything concrete to say, and you are left competing on the same home at a slightly lower price. Writing the benefits down also protects your team: guest services can say yes confidently, because the terms are clear and the same in every market.

### Implementation Steps

#### Part A: Decide What Members Get

1. **Write the benefits list.**
   - Keep only benefits that can be delivered without a site visit. That rules out wine drops and welcome baskets, and keeps anything that can be granted from Hostaway or a phone.
   - Use these as a starting point and adjust the specifics to what you are comfortable committing to:
     - **No platform service fees:** members book direct and save up to 15 percent compared with booking the same home on an OTA. This is already true, and it stays.
     - **Flexible cancellation:** for example, free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival on direct bookings.
     - **Early check-in or late checkout when the calendar allows:** for example, a 1pm checkout whenever the home has no same-day arrival.
     - **Talk to the local team:** a direct line to your guest services team, people who know the home, during stated hours.
     - **First access:** members hear about new homes and peak dates before they open to the platforms, and can reserve the same week next year before anyone else.
   - Write each benefit as a single sentence a guest would understand without reading any small print.
   - [CONFIRM: final cancellation window, late checkout time and guest services hours]

2. **Make the benefits operational in Hostaway.**
   - Create a reservation custom field so the team can see who is a member. In Hostaway, go to Inbox → Manage Custom Fields (or the Custom fields tab under Listings, depending on your dashboard version) and add a dropdown field called "Lifty Life Member" with the values Yes and No.
   - Agree a simple rule for guest services: when a direct booking arrives, check the guest's email against the member list and set the field to Yes if it matches.
   - Build one Hostaway automation for members only. Go to Inbox → Automations → Create new +, choose the Reservation trigger, and under additional conditions set Reservation custom field "Lifty Life Member" is Yes. The message confirms the member benefits for that stay.
   - For late checkout, use the Listing availability condition so the offer only goes out when the home has no arrival on the departure day.
   - Note that Hostaway does not let you edit an automation's conditions once it is created. Test it on one listing before applying it to all of them.

3. **Name it and write the one-page team guide.**
   - Use "Lifty Life Member" throughout. It is free, joining means signing up with an email address, and it applies across every market.
   - Write a one-page guide for guest services: what members get, how to check membership, what to say when someone asks for a perk that is not on the list, and who can approve exceptions.
   - Keep the case-by-case flexibility you already offer for unusual situations. The difference is that the published list becomes the floor, not the ceiling.

#### Part B: Build the Benefits Page

4. **Write the page.**
   - Headline that says who you are and where, for example: "Book direct with the local team behind Harrison's best vacation homes."
   - A short "Who we are" section: a local, BC-based company managing 22 homes in Harrison Hot Springs and 25 more in the surrounding region, plus homes in Whistler, Big White and other BC destinations. Say plainly that you manage the homes yourselves and are not a booking platform.
   - The benefits from Step 1, as five short blocks with an icon or photo each.
   - A simple comparison: "Booking with Lifty Life" against "Booking the same home on a platform," covering fees, who you talk to, cancellation terms and first access. Keep it factual and do not name competitors.
   - Proof: the 4.9-star Google rating from 78 reviews, and three short review quotes that mention communication, location and the full kitchen.
   - A short FAQ: "Is this the same home I saw on Airbnb?", "Why is it cheaper here?", "Are you a booking website?", "How do I use my member benefits?"
   - The sign-up form (Step 5) near the top and again at the bottom.

5. **Add the member sign-up form.**
   - Ask for first name and email only. Add one optional question: "Which places would you like to hear about?" with checkboxes for Harrison Hot Springs, Whistler, Big White and Other BC destinations. This becomes the market tag in Tactic 2.
   - Add an unticked consent checkbox in plain words, for example: "Yes, send me Lifty Life member offers and first access to new homes. Unsubscribe any time." Under Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), the form must identify Lifty Life and include your contact details, and you must keep a record of when and how each person agreed.
   - Connect the form to your email platform so every sign-up is tagged "member" automatically. [CONFIRM: email platform used for the current newsletter]
   - Send each new member to a thank-you page that explains how to use the benefits on the next booking, with a Book Now button to vacation.liftylife.ca.

6. **Publish the page and put it in the main navigation.**
   - Publish at a short, clear address such as liftylife.ca/members. [CONFIRM: page URL]
   - Add a menu item called "Why Book Direct" or "Member Benefits" to the main navigation, and a link in the footer.
   - Set the page title tag to something like "Book Direct with Lifty Life | Local Vacation Rental Team in Harrison Hot Springs and BC."
   - Book a test stay yourself through the page on your phone, from sign-up to the booking engine, and note anywhere you hesitate.

#### Part C: Carry the Message Through the Booking Path

7. **Rework the homepage for guests.**
   - Add a one-line descriptor directly under the Lifty Life logo or hero headline: for example, "Locally managed vacation homes in Harrison Hot Springs and across BC." This addresses visitors who think you are an OTA, without renaming anything.
   - Add a slim benefits strip below the hero with the three strongest benefits and a link to the Benefits page.
   - Move owner-focused content lower on the page or into its own section. You said the site was built for homeowners; the first screen should now speak to guests, with a clear path to "List your property" further down.
   - Update the homepage meta title along the lines the assessment suggested: "Vacation Rentals in Harrison Hot Springs, Whistler & BC | Lifty Life."

8. **Add the member message to the booking engine where Hostaway allows it.**
   - You told us you have limited control over merchandising on the Hostaway booking engine. In the Hostaway dashboard, go to Booking Website → Settings and check what custom text, banners or page content your plan supports.
   - Wherever a banner or custom block is available, add one line: "Lifty Life Members get flexible cancellation and early check-in or late checkout when available. Join free."
   - Do not add direct-booking messages to the shared listing descriptions. Those are likely to sync to Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com, and messages about booking off-platform break Airbnb's rules.
   - If the booking engine allows almost no custom content, make sure every "Book Now" button on liftylife.ca passes through the Benefits page message first, so guests see it before they reach the engine.

9. **Add a sign-up prompt to the main website.**
   - You mentioned pop-ups as something liftylife.ca can support because it is a custom build. Add one sign-up prompt that appears after about 30 seconds or when a visitor moves to leave the page.
   - The message is the membership, not a discount: "Join Lifty Life free for flexible cancellation, late checkout when available and first access to new homes."
   - Show it once per visitor per month at most, and never on the booking engine itself.
   - Connect it to the same email platform and tag as Step 5.

### Tips

- Start with fewer benefits you can keep, not more you might not. One benefit quietly withdrawn will hurt the program more than a short list ever could.

- Use your best Harrison photographs on this page, not stock imagery. The homes are the proof that this is not a generic booking site.
- Keep the owner pitch in mind as you write. A sentence like "our members book direct, so more of every dollar reaches the homeowner" works on both audiences.

## Tactic 2: Guest Follow-Up That Brings People Back Direct
Phase: Retention
Short Label: Guest Follow-Up
Subtitle: A welcome sequence for new members and a post-stay sequence that turns this year's direct guests into next year's direct bookings.

### What We Heard From You

You said the easiest win is recapturing guests who have already stayed with you. You described what happens today: an email captured through the guest portal when the guest provides one, a general newsletter that goes to everyone, and a text asking for a Google review after a five-star Airbnb review. You also told us the details that would make a personal follow-up possible, like an annual fishing trip or an anniversary, end up buried in Hostaway messages, and six months later nobody remembers them. You have reached out personally to a few premium guests, such as a $20,000 Whistler stay, but never as a process across the portfolio. And you said you deliberately avoid telling OTA guests to book direct, because the platforms are too large a share of the business to risk.

### What This Is

Two automated email sequences and the setup beneath them. The welcome sequence goes to every new member who signs up through the Benefits page, explaining what they get and how to use it. The post-stay sequence goes to guests who booked direct or joined as members, thanking them, asking for a Google review, and offering the same dates next year before anyone else. Underneath both sits a simple way to record what guests tell you about their trips, and a rebuilt in-home guest guide that introduces Lifty Life to every guest, including those who booked on a platform.

### Why It Matters

If every guest who booked a second time did so direct, the effect on your bottom line would be significant, and you agreed on the call that it would. Conner has seen next-year rebooking rates of 5 to 10 percent of guests when the request comes the day they check out. That fills part of next summer with no commission and no advertising, a year in advance. It also matters which guests you send to. Airbnb's current Off-Platform Policy prohibits encouraging repeat bookings off Airbnb, using guest contact details for marketing, and asking guests to review an Airbnb stay on another website. This tactic is built around guests you can legally and safely market to, so the Airbnb relationship you depend on is not put at risk.

### Implementation Steps

#### Part A: Set the Ground Rules and the Systems

1. **Sort guests by what you are allowed to send them.**
   - **Direct and website bookings:** full follow-up, including review requests and rebooking offers. Under CASL, a completed purchase gives you implied consent to email for two years.
   - **People who joined as members:** full follow-up, because they gave express consent on the form.
   - **Airbnb and Booking.com bookings:** no marketing emails or texts using the contact details the platform supplied, and no links or offers in platform messages encouraging guests to book off the platform. These guests meet Lifty Life inside the stay instead (Step 9).
   - **VRBO bookings:** VRBO supplies real email addresses, but check whether VRBO's terms allow marketing use before including them.
   - Email your Airbnb and VRBO account managers, describe the plan in a paragraph, and ask for a written reply. You told us they have been relaxed about rate differences before. Get the same clarity here. [CONFIRM: account manager responses]
   - Review the existing text that asks Airbnb reviewers to also leave a Google review. Airbnb's policy specifically prohibits asking guests to review an Airbnb stay on a non-Airbnb website, so raise this with your account manager at the same time.

2. **Connect Hostaway to your email platform and set up the tags.**
   - Check the Hostaway Marketplace for a direct connector to your email platform. If there is none, export direct-booking guest contacts from Hostaway monthly and import them with their booking date recorded as the consent date.
   - Create these tags in your email platform: member, past direct guest, the market they stayed in (Harrison, Whistler, Big White, Other), and occasion (anniversary, annual trip, family holiday).
   - Use the market tag from the Benefits page form for members who have not yet stayed.
   - Retire the single "everyone" newsletter list. From now on, every send goes to a tag.

3. **Capture the trip details that currently get lost.**
   - Add two reservation custom fields in Hostaway: "Occasion" (dropdown: Anniversary, Birthday, Annual trip, Family holiday, Work, Other) and "Trip notes" (text).
   - Ask guest services to fill these in whenever a guest mentions the reason for their trip. Make it part of the check-in message routine, so it takes seconds rather than becoming a separate job.
   - Carry the occasion and the stay dates into the email platform with the contact, so Step 8 can send a reminder ahead of next year's date.
   - Conner mentioned that an MCP connection exists for Hostaway, which would let Claude or ChatGPT read reservation messages and suggest values for these fields. Treat that as a later improvement once the fields are in regular use.

#### Part B: The Welcome Sequence for New Members

4. **Build the first welcome email.**
   - Trigger it the moment someone joins through the Benefits page or the pop-up.
   - Subject along the lines of "Welcome to Lifty Life. Here is what you get."
   - Contents: a short thank you, the member benefits as a short list, and the one thing they need to do to use them: book at vacation.liftylife.ca with the same email address.
   - Sign it from a real person, you or your guest services manager, with a reply-to address that reaches the team. This is the "you talk to people who have been in the home" promise in practice.
   - Include your business name, mailing address and an unsubscribe link in the footer, as CASL requires.

5. **Build the three follow-up welcome emails.**
   - **Day 3, "Who we are":** the story of Lifty Life, the local team, and why you manage homes rather than list them. Two or three photographs of the people as well as the homes.
   - **Day 7, "Where you can go":** the markets, led by Harrison, with three homes the member can book now. Use the market tag so a Whistler sign-up sees Whistler first.
   - **Day 14, "First access":** explain that members hear first about new homes and peak dates, and show what is available for the next season.
   - Keep each email short with one button. Each should make sense on its own, since some members will only open one.

#### Part C: The Post-Stay Sequence for Direct Guests and Members

6. **Build the thank-you and review email.**
   - In Hostaway, go to Inbox → Automations → Create new +, choose the Guest Departure (check-out) trigger, and set it to send one day after checkout.
   - Under Channels, select only your direct and booking website channels, so it never reaches an Airbnb or Booking.com guest.
   - Contents: a personal thank you mentioning the home by name using a dynamic field, a link straight to your Google review form, and one line reminding them they are now a Lifty Life Member if they were not already.
   - Hostaway automated emails for direct and website reservations support HTML formatting, so you can include your logo and a button.

7. **Build the "Where to next?" rebooking email.**
   - A second Hostaway automation on the same trigger and channels, sent three days after checkout.
   - The offer is the same home, same week, next year, reserved now before it opens to the platforms. Include a direct link to that home on vacation.liftylife.ca.
   - Add a second option for guests who want something different: "Or try a Lifty Life home in Whistler or Big White," with one featured home for each.
   - Remind them of the member benefits that apply to the next booking, especially flexible cancellation, which takes the risk out of committing a year ahead.
   - Ask guest services to flag any reply within a business day. A reply here is a booking conversation, not an inbox chore.

8. **Send next-year reminders for anniversaries and annual trips.**
   - Hostaway automations cannot be scheduled more than 60 days after an event, so annual reminders need to run from your email platform instead.
   - Set up a date-based automation in your email platform that sends ten months after the stay date to anyone tagged anniversary, birthday or annual trip.
   - Write it personally: "Last year you spent your anniversary at [home name]. Would you like the same dates again?" If your email platform cannot run date-based automations, pull the list monthly and send these by hand. At current volumes that is a few emails a month.
   - Copy yourself on the first ten sends so you can see how guests respond.

#### Part D: Introduce Lifty Life to Every Guest

9. **Rebuild the in-home guest guide.**
   - You told us the booklet in some homes is both a guest guide and a bit of branding, and that you want to rebuild it. Make the first page about Lifty Life: who you are, that you are local, and how to reach the team.
   - Include a QR code to a Lifty Life local guide page for each market, for example "The Lifty Life Guide to Harrison Hot Springs" with restaurants, walks and rainy-day ideas. Put the member sign-up on that page.
   - For homes that take Airbnb and Booking.com bookings, keep discounts and "book direct and save" messages out of the guide. Present the membership as the local guide plus first access to new homes, and confirm the wording with your account managers before printing.
   - The onboarding summary mentions a Wi-Fi login page as a way to capture guest emails. If you test it, use the same sign-up wording and an unticked consent box, so it can never become a condition of getting online.
   - Print a small run first and put the guide in the ten busiest Harrison homes before rolling it out more widely.

10. **Turn the general newsletter into a member update.**
    - Send it monthly, only to the member and past direct guest tags, and split it by market where possible.
    - Lead with something only members get: a new home before it is listed on the platforms, open dates in a peak week, or a heads-up about next season's calendar.
    - Keep it to three items and one button. A short, useful email is opened; a general newsletter is not.
    - Track bookings from each send by checking new direct reservations from member emails in the week after it goes out.

### Tips

- Send the rebooking email from a person, not "The Lifty Life Team." Guests rebook with people they remember.
- Do not let the post-stay sequence send after a stay with a complaint. Ask guest services to set a Hostaway custom field such as "Guest issue" to Yes when there was a problem, and exclude that value in the automation conditions.
- Resist adding more emails. Four welcome emails and two post-stay emails is enough to test. Look at the replies at Week 8 before writing anything new.
- Keep the account manager replies in writing. If Airbnb's policy changes, you will want to show you asked first and followed what you were told.

## Tactic 3: Fill the Off-Season With Extended Stays
Phase: Off-Season Fill
Short Label: Extended Stays
Subtitle: A dedicated page and a short outbound list that make Lifty Life the obvious furnished-housing partner for crews and relocations in Harrison and Agassiz.

### What We Heard From You

You told us summer fills itself and the real challenge is October. You already host some longer stays, including insurance relocations for families whose homes were damaged, and you have connected with Enbridge about the pipeline work in Agassiz, the neighbouring community. Your homes suit this market: full kitchens, laundry and space for a month or two. You said most of this business arrives inbound today, and you asked how to do more outbound and encourage these guests to book direct, where you can offer flexible terms like monthly payments instead of paying everything upfront. You also pointed out that filling off-season calendars this way is something few managers offer, and that it matters to homeowners as much as to guests.

### What This Is

One landing page built for furnished stays of a month or more in Harrison Hot Springs and Agassiz, aimed at the people who arrange them: site managers, contractors, insurance adjusters and relocation coordinators. It sets out which homes suit longer stays, what is included, and the terms you can offer direct, with an enquiry form. Alongside it, a short outbound list of local employers and insurance contacts, each receiving one email and one follow-up. The page also becomes the destination for the extended-stay Google Ads campaign in Tactic 4.

### Why It Matters

Extended-stay guests are the bookings OTAs serve worst and you serve best. A contractor housing three workers for two months needs invoicing, monthly payments and someone who answers the phone, none of which a booking platform provides. Conner pointed out on the call that searches like "furnished rental Agassiz" are too specialised for the big platforms to buy, so a small, focused page can be the first result. Every month filled this way is commission-free, falls in the season you most need to fill, and gives you a strong line for the next homeowner pitch: "we fill your home in October."

### Implementation Steps

#### Part A: Define the Offer

1. **Choose the homes and the guests.**
   - Start with Harrison Hot Springs and Agassiz, where the Enbridge work and most of your inventory sit. Add Big White as a second page later if this one produces enquiries.
   - From your Hostaway listings, pick the homes that suit stays of 30 nights or more: full kitchen, in-suite laundry, parking for work vehicles, reliable Wi-Fi and a table someone can work at.
   - Ask each owner of those homes to confirm they are happy to accept monthly guests. Some may not be, and it is better to know before the page goes live.
   - Write down the two or three guest types you are designing for: work crews on regional projects, insurance relocations, and other professionals on temporary assignments.
   - [CONFIRM: Enbridge project timing in Agassiz]

2. **Set the extended-stay terms.**
   - Decide the minimum stay. Thirty nights is common for this market, though you may prefer 14 nights in the off-season.
   - Write the terms you can offer only on direct bookings: monthly payments rather than full prepayment, invoicing to an employer or insurer, a mid-stay clean on a set schedule, and flexible extensions.
   - Set a monthly rate approach for each home, or a rule such as a percentage off the nightly rate for stays of 30 nights or more. [CONFIRM: extended-stay pricing rule]
   - Decide who answers extended-stay enquiries and how fast. A same-business-day reply is the standard to aim for in this market.

#### Part B: Build the Page

3. **Write the extended stays page.**
   - Headline that names the place and the need, for example: "Furnished Monthly Rentals in Harrison Hot Springs and Agassiz."

## Tactic 4: Google Ads for Harrison Direct Bookings
Phase: Paid Acquisition
Short Label: Google Ads
Subtitle: Track direct bookings properly, then buy the Harrison and extended-stay searches the big platforms ignore, judged against the $150 commission each booking would otherwise cost.

### What We Heard From You

You told us the battle is not convincing people to leave Airbnb. It is being visible to someone searching for a place to stay in Harrison Hot Springs who has not chosen a platform and is looking at hotels. You believe your homes are better than most of what is in town, and that the hotels are a bit tired. You also gave us the economics yourself: of a $1,000 booking, $150 goes to Airbnb, and a $50 cost to win the booking directly would be far better. You said SEO used to be an advantage but AI search results have levelled the field, and the questionnaire confirms you are not running paid search today. You also offered Junction access to Google Analytics if needed.

### What This Is

Two small Google Search campaigns, set up after the tracking is fixed. The first targets people searching for accommodation in Harrison Hot Springs and sends them to pages that explain why booking with Lifty Life is better. The second targets furnished and monthly rental searches around Harrison and Agassiz and sends them to the extended stays page from Tactic 3. Before either goes live, Google Analytics 4 is connected to the Hostaway booking engine, so every direct booking, member sign-up and enquiry is counted.

### Why It Matters

Without tracking, you cannot tell whether a direct booking came from an ad, the newsletter or a returning guest, and you would be spending money you cannot judge. With tracking, the test becomes simple and uses your own numbers: if Google Ads wins a Harrison booking for less than the 15 percent commission it would have cost on a platform, it is worth scaling. Paid search is also the fastest way to reach first-time Harrison visitors. The repeat-guest work in Tactic 2 cannot reach them because they have not stayed yet.

### Implementation Steps

#### Part A: Fix Measurement First

1. **Connect Google Analytics 4 to the Hostaway booking engine.**
   - Confirm which GA4 property is installed on liftylife.ca and use that same property for the booking engine, so a visitor's journey is counted as one visit across both domains. [CONFIRM: GA4 property installed on liftylife.ca]
   - In the Hostaway dashboard, go to Booking Website → Settings → Advanced, paste the GA4 Measurement ID into the Google Analytics Tracking ID field, and save.
   - In GA4, go to Admin → Data streams → your web stream → Configure tag settings → Configure your domains, and add both liftylife.ca and vacation.liftylife.ca so the move from one to the other is not counted as a new visit.
   - Make a test booking (or start one) and confirm the events appear in the GA4 Realtime report. Hostaway sends Listings Search, Book now click, Send inquiry click and Purchase by default.

2. **Set up the key events and the revenue figure.**
   - In GA4, go to Admin → Data display → Events and mark purchase as a key event. This is your direct booking.
   - Mark the member sign-up (from Tactic 1) and extended_stay_enquiry (from Tactic 3) as key events as well. Ask whoever maintains liftylife.ca to fire an event called member_signup when the Benefits page form or pop-up is submitted.
   - Under Admin → Custom definitions, create a custom metric for reservationTotalAmount (currency) and custom dimensions for listingName and reservationCouponId, which Hostaway already sends. This lets you see revenue by home and track any member coupon codes later.
   - Do not mark page views or first visits as key events. They fire on every page and make every report meaningless.

3. **Record the baseline before any money is spent.**
   - Export one page of numbers dated to the end of Week 3: sessions on liftylife.ca and the booking engine, purchases and their value, member sign-ups at zero, and Harrison's direct share of bookings from Hostaway reports.
   - Note the date the tracking went live. Reports before and after will not be comparable.
   - Send a copy to Junction ahead of the 30-day follow-up call.

#### Part B: Decide the Budget and Build the Account

4. **Set the budget against the commission you already pay.**
   - Treat the ad budget as commission you are choosing to spend differently. You currently pay roughly $100,000 a year in OTA commission; a test of a few hundred dollars a month is a small fraction of that.
   - A reasonable starting point for two small search campaigns in one town is $15 to $30 a day in total, adjusted after the first four weeks. [CONFIRM: monthly Google Ads budget]
   - Write down your break-even figure before launching. At a $1,000 booking and 15 percent commission, any cost per direct booking under $150 beats the platforms, and anything near your $50 target is a clear win.
   - Decide who checks the account each week. It needs about 20 minutes a week once running, and it is the one part of this plan that cannot be left alone.

5. **Create the Google Ads account and link it to GA4.**
   - At ads.google.com, choose "Switch to Expert Mode" on the first screen rather than accepting a Smart Campaign, which gives Google control of targeting in ways that rarely suit a small budget.
   - Use the same Google account that owns GA4 and your Google Business Profile, which makes linking straightforward.
   - Set the currency to CAD and the time zone to Pacific. Neither can be changed later without opening a new account.
   - Link GA4 under Tools → Data manager, then import purchase, member_signup and extended_stay_enquiry as conversions. Set purchase as the primary conversion for the Harrison campaign and extended_stay_enquiry as primary for the extended-stay campaign.

#### Part C: Build the Two Campaigns

6. **Build the Harrison accommodation campaign.**
   - One Search campaign. Switch off the Display Network and search partners, both of which are on by default.
   - Target the Lower Mainland, the Fraser Valley and northwest Washington State, which matches your guest profile of Metro Vancouver and Seattle-Bellingham weekenders. Use "Presence: people in or regularly in your targeted locations" rather than the default.
   - Ad group one, rental intent: harrison hot springs vacation rentals, harrison hot springs cabin rental, harrison lake rentals, harrison hot springs house rental.
   - Ad group two, general accommodation intent: harrison hot springs accommodation, places to stay in harrison hot springs, where to stay harrison hot springs. You noted that most people search "hotel" rather than "accommodation," so add harrison hot springs hotels as a third, separate ad group and watch it closely, since it will cost more and convert differently.
   - Do not bid on the names of other businesses in town.
   - Use phrase match. Write ten headlines and four descriptions per ad group, including "Local Team in Harrison," "Lake and Mountain View Homes," "Rated 4.9 Stars on Google," and "Flexible Member Cancellation."
   - Send traffic to a Harrison page on liftylife.ca that shows Harrison homes with the member benefits strip, rather than to the homepage. If no such page exists yet, use the Benefits page until one is built. [CONFIRM: Harrison landing page URL]

7. **Build the extended-stay campaign.**
   - A second Search campaign targeting British Columbia, since the people booking crew housing are often based elsewhere in the province.
   - Keywords: furnished rental agassiz, monthly rental harrison hot springs, furnished monthly rental fraser valley, corporate housing agassiz, temporary housing harrison hot springs, insurance relocation housing fraser valley.
   - Send all traffic to the extended stays page from Tactic 3, and optimise toward extended_stay_enquiry.
   - Headlines should name the place and the terms: "Furnished Homes Near Agassiz," "Monthly Payments Available," "Crew & Relocation Housing."
   - Expect few clicks and high value. One booking for a crew of three for two months can justify months of spend on this campaign.

8. **Add the negative keywords before launch.**
   - Add negatives for searches that will never book: jobs, employment, for sale, real estate, mortgage, camping, campground, rv park, free, cheap.
   - On the extended-stay campaign, also add negatives for long-term unfurnished renting: apartment for rent, long term lease, basement suite.
   - Review this list on day three of each campaign, then weekly.

#### Part D: Review Weekly and Decide at Day 90

9. **Run a 20-minute weekly review.**
   - Open the Search terms report and add any irrelevant searches as negative keywords.
   - Check cost per purchase and cost per enquiry against the break-even figure from Step 4.
   - Pause any keyword that reaches 40 clicks with no purchase, member sign-up or enquiry.
   - Note the week's figures in one line of the same sheet you use for Tactic 3, so the trend is visible at a glance.

10. **Make the Week 12 decision and write it down.**
    - Compare the results with the Week 3 baseline: direct purchases, member sign-ups, enquiries and cost per booking.
    - If the Harrison campaign is winning bookings below $100 each, increase its budget for the spring planning season, when Harrison summer stays get booked. If it is above $150, cut it back and move the money to the extended-stay campaign or pause.
    - Remember that bookings often happen days after the first click. Use GA4's attribution reports, not only the Google Ads conversion column, before deciding.
    - Record the decision and the numbers behind it, so the spring budget is set on evidence.

- Search your own keywords on your phone after launch, but do not click your own ads. It costs money and skews the numbers.
