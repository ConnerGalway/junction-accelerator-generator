## Tourism Context

### Visitor Profile
Red Cariboo Resort attracts outdoor adventure travelers, fly-fishing enthusiasts, wilderness seekers, and international tourists (especially German-speaking based on website language support) visiting the Chilcotin region for multi-day wilderness experiences, typically aged 35-65 with moderate-to-high income seeking authentic remote Canadian experiences.

### Discovery Journey
Visitors discover Anahim Lake resorts through:
1. Google searches for "Chilcotin accommodations", "Dean River fishing lodges", "BC wilderness resorts"
2. Trip planning for Highway 20 road trips
3. Fly-fishing forums and outdoor adventure blogs
4. Word-of-mouth from previous guests

Digital presence is critical as this is a destination requiring significant advance planning (8-hour drive from major centers).

### Seasonal Considerations
- Peak season June-September for fishing, hiking, and wildlife viewing
- Digital strategy should emphasize booking windows 3-6 months in advance
- Winter months for promoting summer availability
- Shoulder seasons (May, October) need specific content addressing weather/accessibility
- Social content should be year-round to maintain engagement even during closed periods

### Trip Integration
Red Cariboo Resort is a destination anchor in multi-day Chilcotin itineraries. Visitors typically combine with:
- Tweedsmuir Provincial Park
- Highway 20 scenic drives
- Charlotte Alplands
- Dean River activities

Digital presence should emphasize "base camp" positioning and nearby attractions, cross-promote with regional tourism partners, provide itinerary suggestions.
